# Information has wet and dry states too

The "dry" and "wet" states of information can be understood as dehydration and rehydration. Different situations need different concentrations: written for a person to read, information should be wetter, with context, rhetoric and complete expression; kept for archive, it can be dehydrated somewhat, keeping only the information itself and lightening the weight of long-term storage.
